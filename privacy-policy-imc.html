This Privacy Policy explains how BMI Control (the "app"), published by S.M.A. - Simplified Mobile Apps, handles information. The app is free and supported by ads shown through Google AdMob. We do not require an account, and we do not operate a server that receives your data.

1. Target audience

This app is not directed to children. It is intended for users over 16 years of age.

2. Information handled on your device

We (the developer) do not collect your personal information. The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or social network accounts.

  • Measurements you enter: Values such as height, weight and the resulting BMI are processed on your device. We do not send them to our servers, and we do not share them with advertisers.

3. Data collected automatically by third-party services

This app contains ads served through the Google AdMob platform, which uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK. According to Google, this SDK automatically collects and shares the following data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address: It may be used to estimate the general (approximate) location of your device. This is not your GPS location.
  • User product interactions: For example app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic information: Information about the performance of the app and the SDK, such as launch time, hang rate and energy usage.
  • Device and account identifiers: The Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, if applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device.

This data is collected and processed by Google as described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Google AdMob policy. Data collected by the SDK is encrypted in transit. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use this data for any purpose other than showing ads, measuring them and preventing fraud.

4. Google Play and Play Console statistics

Google Play and the Google Play Console also collect information from devices, such as installs, crashes, performance and device model. Google collects it under its own privacy policy. As the developer, we only see aggregated statistics and do not receive information that identifies you.

5. Advertising choices

You can control ads on your device at any time:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Turn off ad personalization through Google My Ad Center.
  • Where required by applicable law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice later.

6. Security

Data transmitted by the advertising and analytics services is encrypted in transit. Data that stays on your device is protected by the Android app sandbox and your device's security settings.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device: It is kept until you delete it, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all local data.
  • Data held by Google: Advertising, analytics and diagnostic data is retained and managed by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy. We do not keep copies of it.
  • Requests: The app does not have user accounts. For any question or request about your data, write to simplified.maed@gmail.com.

8. Social networks

This app does not interact with, request permissions for, or link to your social network accounts.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to certain processing. Because we do not hold your personal data, most requests are handled through the controls described above and by Google. You can also contact us at simplified.maed@gmail.com.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or the services it uses change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
